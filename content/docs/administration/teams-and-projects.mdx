title: Teams and Projects

Teams and Projects are the installation's shared workspaces. They work the same way; the two kinds exist so you can organise by both, for example a Team per department and a Project per grant. A Project never belongs to a Team, and membership of one gives nothing in the other.

Only Platform Admins create them, in **Admin › Teams** and **Admin › Projects**.

## Create one

Give it a **name** and an **initial owner**, who must already have a platform role. The owner can then add members, admins and service accounts on the workspace's **Members** page. People don't have to be members of anything to get their personal workspace.

## A Team's or Project's page

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Members, available catalogs, models available and cost center at a glance. |
| **Members** | Members and their roles and sources. Platform Admins can add, change and remove manual memberships here too, including owners; the last owner can't be removed. |
| **Models** | The models it can use, the catalogs it gets, and direct assignments. |
| **Limits** | The type default, and an optional override for this workspace. |
| **Access** | Why each model is or isn't usable, layer by layer. |
| **General** | Name, cost center, and disabling the workspace. |

A Platform Admin who isn't a member sees configuration here, not the workspace's keys or requests.

## Overrides for one workspace

Each kind of workspace takes its **defaults** from Admin: default catalogs (see [Catalogs](/docs/administration/catalogs)) and default limits (see [Limits and budgets](/docs/administration/limits-and-budgets)). Workspaces without an override follow changes to the defaults at once.

For one workspace you can **replace** either:

- **Catalogs**: choose exactly which catalogs it gets. This replaces the type's defaults; it doesn't add to them. An empty replacement means no catalog models. **Reset catalog inheritance** goes back to the defaults.
- **Limits**: set a platform override for its rate limits, jobs at once, storage and budgets. This replaces the type default entirely, even fields you leave blank. **Reset policy inheritance** goes back to the defaults.

You can also **assign a model directly**, outside any catalog. Direct assignments survive catalog changes, and the workspace's admins still choose whether to use them.

## Cost centers

A cost center is an optional label for chargeback, such as "Chemistry" with the code `CHEM`. Several workspaces can share one. Create them in **Admin › Cost centers**, then assign one with **Assign a team or project** there, or on the workspace's **General** tab in Admin.

- Every request records the workspace's cost center **at the moment it was admitted**. Changing or removing the assignment, or renaming the cost center, only affects new requests; history keeps its original label.
- Usage without a cost center is tracked in full as **Unallocated**.
- Archiving a cost center clears its current assignments and keeps it on past records.
- Cost centers never give anyone access to anything.

## Disable a workspace

**Disable workspace…** (in the page's menu, or on the **General** tab) refuses new requests and revokes the workspace's keys. Its history, costs and audit records are kept, and its pages stay readable but read-only. **Enable workspace** makes it usable again, but revoked keys don't come back: issue new ones.
