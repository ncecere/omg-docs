## What admins can and can't see

Platform roles give authority over the installation, not over people's private work:

- **Personal workspaces** stay private. Admins and Auditors see a personal workspace's owner, its usage, cost and storage **totals** in reports, and the limits and catalogs set for it, never its keys, requests, sessions or files. Holds, scheduled reports and provider reconciliation combine personal workspaces into one row without identities. On the workspace's own pages, another person's personal workspace answers as if it didn't exist.
- **Teams and Projects**: admins and Auditors see their configuration, members and cost totals. Admin › Logs shows their requests' metadata. Their keys and request details inside the workspace's own pages need a membership, like anyone else.
- **Making a key** in a Team or Project needs a real membership, even for a Platform Admin.

Auditors see every Admin page read-only, except **Admin API tokens**, which they can't open: no buttons that change anything, and the API refuses changes.
