## What admins can and can't see

Platform roles give authority over the installation, not over people's private work:

- **Personal workspaces** stay private. Admins and Auditors see their cost and usage **totals** in reports, never their keys or requests.
- **Teams and Projects**: admins and Auditors see their configuration, members and cost totals. Admin › Logs shows their requests' metadata. Their keys and request details inside the workspace's own pages need a membership, like anyone else.
- **Making a key** in a Team or Project needs a real membership, even for a Platform Admin.

Auditors see every Admin page read-only: no buttons that change anything, and the API refuses changes.
