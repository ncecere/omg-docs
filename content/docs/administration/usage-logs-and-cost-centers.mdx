The **Usage & spend** group in Admin covers the whole installation. Platform Admins and Auditors see the same figures.

## Usage & costs

Admin's **Usage & costs** has the same **Overview** and **Explore** views as a workspace's (see [Usage and costs](/docs/using/usage-and-costs)), across every workspace, plus **By workspace**: one row per workspace with its spend, requests and tokens, its cost center, and actions to adjust it. Personal workspaces are named **Personal · *owner*** and show totals only.

<Screenshot slot="admin-usage" />

- **Personal workspaces count in every total**, but only as totals. Their keys are grouped into one "Personal workspace keys" row, and members' rows show per-person totals only. No personal request or key is ever listed.
- There is no installation-wide budget to show here. Add an [Installation spend alert](/docs/administration/alerts#installation-spend) to be notified when total spend crosses a reference amount.
- Filter by workspace, model, key, member, status, cost center or service account. Periods are 1 to 93 UTC days.
- The **By workspace** row menu has shortcuts: assign a future cost center, assign a direct model, replace catalog availability, set custom limits, and reset catalog or policy inheritance.

From v0.9.0 Admin's Usage & costs also has the **Holds**, **Scheduled reports** and, from v0.10.0, **Provider reconciliation** tabs below.

### Holds

**Holds** lists requests whose cost has been **unknown for 24 hours or more**, oldest first, across every Team and Project: the workspace, model, provider, how long it has been unknown, and the known floor of the hold (at least this much; not an upper limit). Holds with no known upper limit say **Unbounded**. Personal workspaces appear only as one combined row, **Personal workspaces, combined**, with no workspace, owner or request; a small count can still describe one person.

<Screenshot slot="admin-holds" />

The tab only reads. For a finished request with a pinned price, a Platform Admin can choose **Reconcile** to record the provider's usage as evidence and resolve its cost ([Pricing](/docs/administration/pricing#cost-evidence-and-reconciliation)). Nothing here refunds, releases or writes off a hold. The built-in [aged unknown-hold alert](/docs/administration/alerts#built-in-alerts) links here.

### Scheduled reports

**Scheduled reports** emails a link to a weekly or monthly cost report. In Admin, Platform Admins schedule reports for the whole installation or one cost center (up to 100 such schedules in total); Auditors can read them but not change them. Workspace owners and admins schedule their own Team or Project reports, and people their personal ones, from the workspace's Usage & costs ([Usage and costs](/docs/using/usage-and-costs)).

<Screenshot slot="admin-scheduled-reports" />

- A schedule starts **paused**. Choose weekly or monthly, a time zone and a local time for the email, and up to 50 recipients, picked by their user id; then resume it. The time only decides when the email goes out: the reported period is always a whole UTC week (Monday to Monday) or calendar month.
- The email holds a link only: no amounts, names or attachments. Every recipient must sign in, and every view and download checks their access again.
- A report is an aggregate snapshot of the period's estimated cost, holds and unresolved requests, with up to 100 rows per breakdown and the untruncated totals. Personal workspaces are combined into one row. **Download aggregate CSV** exports the snapshot, up to 1,000 rows.
- Snapshots expire after 90 days.
- Sending needs the [email relay](/docs/administration/settings#email) and an HTTPS public URL set by the operator. If the operator sets `GATEWAY_ALERT_INTERVAL_SECONDS=0`, reports are still generated but not emailed.
- Admin API tokens can't use scheduled reports.

### Provider reconciliation

**Provider reconciliation** compares what a provider reported charging with the gateway's own estimates, for a closed period. In v0.10.0 it supports **Anthropic** cost reports in US dollars only. It's a check on your configured prices, not a verified invoice or a payable balance. Platform Admins and Auditors read frozen comparisons; only Platform Admins see **Upload & manage provider costs**. Admin API tokens can't use it.

1. **Upload report**: the Anthropic Cost Report JSON for a complete, closed period, up to 5 MiB and 50,000 lines. Uploading the same file again returns the existing import.
2. **Mappings**: bind each provider workspace to one Anthropic connection for a UTC date range, with an evidence reference, and attest the whole mapping revision. A provider workspace fed by several connections must stay shared and unallocated. Saving creates permanent history.
3. **Freeze reconciliation** for the period. The frozen comparison never changes afterwards: later usage, mappings and imports don't recompute it, and it changes no costs, holds, budgets or prices.

A comparison shows the provider's charges and the variance (credits can make them negative), with **Unknown** or **Incomplete** where a comparison isn't possible, never an invented zero. Holds, unresolved coverage and personal totals (combined, without identities) are listed separately and never added to the charges.

## Logs

Admin's **Logs** is the same view as a workspace's [Logs](/docs/using/logs), over every Team and Project, with a **Workspace** column and filter. **Finish reason** is hidden by default; turn it on under **Columns**. Personal workspaces never appear as rows: their activity stays in Usage & costs totals. **Batches** lists Team and Project batches, with running, finished and failed totals for personal workspaces.

<Screenshot slot="admin-logs" />

- **Refresh** (from v0.8.0) reloads the current tab's rows and totals with the same filters; Logs doesn't poll on its own.
- A request's page shows a **Trace ID** (from v0.8.0). If the operator has turned on [tracing](/docs/self-hosting/metrics-and-monitoring#opentelemetry-exports), copy it to find the request's trace in your trace store; an id alone doesn't mean the request was sampled.
- The **Sessions** tab, when empty, explains that requests without a session id are under **Requests**.

Remember that session ids sent by clients (`X-Session-Id`, or the `user` field) are visible here for Teams and Projects.

## Storage usage

Storage in the file store is recorded hourly per workspace and purpose, and shown in GB-days (1 GiB kept for 24 hours). Admins see per-workspace totals; personal workspaces show totals only. Storage is **Not charged**: it has no price yet.

## Cost centers

**Cost centers** label spending for chargeback, such as a department's code. **Create cost center** with a name and code, then **Assign a team or project**. You can also set it on a Team's or Project's **General** tab, or with **Assign future cost center…** in Usage & costs › By workspace.

- A request takes the workspace's cost center **when it is admitted**. Changes and renames apply to new requests only; history keeps the label it had.
- Usage with no cost center is **Unallocated**, and fully counted.
- **Archive cost center…** clears current assignments; past usage keeps the label.
- Cost centers give no access to anything.

Reports can filter and group by cost center, including `unallocated`.

## Exports

Workspace admins can export a workspace's cost records as CSV, up to 1,000 rows per page, and Explore tables as CSV. There is no installation-wide export of individual requests: platform reports are aggregates.

## Reading reports correctly

- **Settled** cost and amounts **on hold** are shown separately. Don't add holds as extra spend.
- Health counts (pending, unknown, unpriced, unbounded, aged holds) overlap: one request can be in several.
- Reports are live estimates from configured prices, not frozen statements or invoices.
