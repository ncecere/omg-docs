The **Usage & spend** group in Admin covers the whole installation. Platform Admins and Auditors see the same figures.

## Usage & costs

Admin's **Usage & costs** has the same **Overview** and **Explore** views as a workspace's (see [Usage and costs](/docs/using/usage-and-costs)), across every workspace, plus **By workspace**: one row per workspace with its spend, requests and tokens, its cost center, and actions to adjust it. Personal workspaces are named **Personal · *owner*** and show totals only.

<Screenshot slot="admin-usage" />

- **Personal workspaces count in every total**, but only as totals. Their keys are grouped into one "Personal workspace keys" row, and members' rows show per-person totals only. No personal request or key is ever listed.
- There is no installation-wide budget to show here. Add an [Installation spend alert](/docs/administration/alerts#installation-spend) to be notified when total spend crosses a reference amount.
- Filter by workspace, model, key, member, status, cost center or service account. Periods are 1 to 93 UTC days.
- The **By workspace** row menu has shortcuts: assign a future cost center, assign a direct model, replace catalog availability, set custom limits, and reset catalog or policy inheritance.

## Logs

Admin's **Logs** is the same view as a workspace's [Logs](/docs/using/logs), over every Team and Project, with a **Workspace** column and filter. **Finish reason** is hidden by default; turn it on under **Columns**. Personal workspaces never appear as rows: their activity stays in Usage & costs totals. **Batches** lists Team and Project batches, with running, finished and failed totals for personal workspaces.

<Screenshot slot="admin-logs" />

Remember that session ids sent by clients (`X-Session-Id`, or the `user` field) are visible here for Teams and Projects.

## Storage usage

Storage in the file store is recorded hourly per workspace and purpose, and shown in GB-days (1 GiB kept for 24 hours). Admins see per-workspace totals; personal workspaces show totals only. Storage is **Not charged**: it has no price yet.

## Cost centers

**Cost centers** label spending for chargeback, such as a department's code. **Create cost center** with a name and code, then **Assign a team or project**. You can also set it on a Team's or Project's **General** tab, or with **Assign future cost center…** in Usage & costs › By workspace.

- A request takes the workspace's cost center **when it is admitted**. Changes and renames apply to new requests only; history keeps the label it had.
- Usage with no cost center is **Unallocated**, and fully counted.
- **Archive cost center…** clears current assignments; past usage keeps the label.
- Cost centers give no access to anything.

Reports can filter and group by cost center, including `unallocated`.

## Exports

Workspace admins can export a workspace's cost records as CSV, up to 1,000 rows per page, and Explore tables as CSV. There is no installation-wide export of individual requests: platform reports are aggregates.

## Reading reports correctly

- **Settled** cost and amounts **on hold** are shown separately. Don't add holds as extra spend.
- Health counts (pending, unknown, unpriced, unbounded, aged holds) overlap: one request can be in several.
- Reports are live estimates from configured prices, not frozen statements or invoices.
