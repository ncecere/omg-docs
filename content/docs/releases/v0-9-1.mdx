Released 2026-10-26. No migrations.

A security and maintenance patch after [v0.9.0](/docs/releases/v0-9-0). It adds no migrations and no provider capabilities.

## A new runtime image

The image moves from the deprecated Debian 12 distroless runtime to a supported Debian 13 one, pinned by digest. The old runtime's OpenSSL was affected by CVE-2026-84782; the new one has `libssl3t64 3.5.7-1~deb13u3`. The image still runs as UID and GID `10001`, with no shell and a read-only filesystem.

**The CA trust store changes with it.** Before you roll out, check your outbound TLS connections (providers, sign-in, S3, SMTP, audit export and collectors) against the new image, especially private or older certificate chains.

## Stricter image publication

Image builds and scans run in read-only jobs; the jobs that push to the registry accept only checked, immutable images and checksum-verified tools. Each architecture keeps its full SBOM and provenance, and the exact built digest is what gets signed. Signing uses cosign 3.1.4; verify with cosign v3.0 or newer, as before. See [Container and Compose](/docs/self-hosting/container#release-images).

## Dashboard fixes

- A workspace you can't open shows a neutral notice, without rendering the page or showing its identifiers. The notice clears when you navigate and isn't shown again after a reload.
- Technical setup guidance for scheduled reports is shown only to Platform Admins. Narrow report tabs can be scrolled to, and route change previews are readable and keyboard-scrollable.
- Holds and log links have accessible names that match their visible text, and tab, filter and command-palette controls are refreshed.

## Upgrade guide

No migrations or runtime grant changes: the schema still ends at `0067`. From v0.9.0, check your outbound TLS against the new image, then replace the image by digest and restart with the normal drain.

Coming from an earlier release, first follow the [v0.9.0 upgrade guide](/docs/releases/v0-9-0#upgrade-guide).

## Known limitations

- The steady-load stall and an intermittent `503` in one accounting test are still unexplained. New measurements don't establish their cause or certify capacity.
- The [v0.9.0 limitations](/docs/releases/v0-9-0#known-limitations) still apply.
