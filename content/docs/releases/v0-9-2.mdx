Released 2026-10-27. No migrations.

A maintenance patch after [v0.9.1](/docs/releases/v0-9-1). It adds no features, dependencies or security fixes.

## Fixes

- **`grants verify` uses its own test history.** The privilege check could collide with real open incidents, such as a personal aged-hold alert or the SCIM last-admin alert. It now uses its own closed test records. It doesn't change real incidents or holds, and it checks the same grants and refusals as before.

The release also adds test-only diagnostics for accounting failures; production behaviour is unchanged.

## Upgrade guide

No migrations or runtime grant changes: the schema still ends at `0067`. From v0.9.1, replace the image by digest and restart with the normal drain.

Coming from an earlier release, first follow the [v0.9.1 upgrade guide](/docs/releases/v0-9-1#upgrade-guide), including its outbound TLS check, and the [v0.9.0 upgrade guide](/docs/releases/v0-9-0#upgrade-guide).

## Known limitations

- The cause of an intermittent `accounting_unavailable` in tests, and the steady-load stall, are still unexplained.
- The [v0.9.0 limitations](/docs/releases/v0-9-0#known-limitations) still apply.
