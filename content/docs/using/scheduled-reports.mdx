---
title: Scheduled reports
description: Weekly or monthly cost reports for a Team, Project or personal workspace, with an email link when each is ready.
---

A scheduled report is a snapshot of a workspace's costs for one week or one month, made automatically when the period ends. The people you choose get an email with a link to it. Reports are in **Usage & costs › Scheduled reports**.

## Who can use them

| Workspace | Who can create, change and read reports | Who can receive them |
|---|---|---|
| Team or Project | Its owners and admins | 1 to 50 of its current owners and admins |
| Personal | Its owner | The owner only |

Members of a Team or Project don't see the tab. A platform role alone gives no access to a workspace's reports. Platform Admins have their own reports for the installation and for cost centers in **Admin › Usage & costs**.

A Team or Project can have up to 50 schedules, and a person up to 10 for their personal workspace.

## Create a schedule

1. Open **Usage & costs**, then **Scheduled reports**, and choose **Create schedule**.
2. Fill in the form:

   | Field | What it does |
   |---|---|
   | **Cadence** | **Weekly** (Monday to Monday) or **Monthly** (calendar months). Periods are always in UTC. |
   | **IANA time zone** | The time zone for delivery, such as `America/New_York`. It changes when the email goes out, not which days the report covers. |
   | **Local delivery time** | `HH:MM`, 24-hour time. The email goes out at the first such time after the period ends. |
   | **Recipient user IDs** | Team or Project only: 1 to 50 people's user IDs (not email addresses), separated by commas or new lines. Your own ID is filled in. To find someone's, open **Settings › Members** and choose **Copy user ID** in their row. |
   | **Historical cost center ID** | Team or Project only, optional: report only the requests that were attributed to that cost center when they were made. It can't be changed later. |

3. Choose **Create schedule**. Every schedule starts **Paused**.
4. Check the schedule in the list, then choose **Resume** to make it **Active**.

A schedule only makes reports for periods that end after you resume it: there is no catch-up of earlier periods. **Edit schedule** changes the cadence, time zone, delivery time and recipients for future reports. **Pause** stops new reports and unsent emails; **Delete** is final, and links to its reports stop working.

<Callout title="Email must be set up">
  Reports are emailed only when a Platform Admin has set up email (**Admin › Settings › Email**). Until then, the tab shows **Email delivery unavailable**; reports are still made and readable here, with the delivery status **Email not configured**.
</Callout>

## Read a report

The list shows each schedule's next report and state. Under **Recent runs**, choose a schedule to see its last 20 reports, with their UTC period, delivery status (such as **Delivered**, **Delivery pending** or **Email not configured**) and when each expires. Select a period to open the report.

A report shows:

- **Full totals**: the settled configured-rate estimate, the held floor (money still on hold), and attempt counts, including unresolved attempts;
- **Coverage**: how many attempts were priced, settled, pending or unknown;
- breakdowns, such as by model, provider and cost center, up to 100 entries each (the totals above always cover everything);
- measures a report can't compute, marked **Unavailable**, never as zero.

**Download aggregate CSV** saves the same figures as a spreadsheet. **Refresh report** checks your access again.

<Screenshot slot="scheduled-report-run" />

- A report is a fixed snapshot. Later corrections to costs don't change it.
- Its amounts are estimates from configured prices, not invoices, and held amounts are floors, not extra charges.
- Reports and their CSV files are kept for 90 days, then only their dates and status remain.

## What the email contains

Only a notice that a report is ready, the UTC period and a link. It never contains amounts, names, the recipient list or an attachment. Opening the link needs you to sign in, and the gateway checks again that you may read the report: a forwarded link doesn't give anyone access. If you have since lost access, for example by leaving the workspace, the report is unavailable to you.

Recipients who lose their access stop receiving the emails. If the person who created a schedule loses access, the schedule stops making reports.
