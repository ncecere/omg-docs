## The short version

1. Pick a workspace in the sidebar: your **Personal** workspace, or a Team or Project you belong to.
2. Open **Models** to see which models the workspace can use, and copy a model's name.
3. Open **API keys**, choose **Create key**, and save the key somewhere safe. It is shown once.
4. Point the OpenAI or Anthropic SDK at your gateway's address with that key, and use the model's name.
5. Watch **Logs** and **Usage & costs** to see what your requests did and what they cost.

If a model you need isn't there, ask your workspace's admin (for a Team or Project) or a Platform Admin (for your personal workspace).
