---
title: Joining a Team or Project
description: Ask to join a discoverable Team or Project, follow and withdraw your requests, and accept an invitation.
---

There are three ways into a Team or Project: an owner or admin adds you, you accept an invitation, or you ask to join one that is discoverable. Each needs a platform role first: joining a workspace never gives you one.

## Ask to join

**Request access**, in the sidebar's **Workspace** group, lists the Teams and Projects you can ask to join: those a Platform Admin made discoverable and that you aren't a member of yet. Personal workspaces and Teams or Projects that aren't discoverable never appear.

<Screenshot slot="request-access" />

1. Open **Request access**.
2. In the workspace's row menu, choose **Request access…**.
3. Optionally give a **Reason**: up to 1,000 characters, on one line.
4. Choose **Send request**.

The workspace's owners and admins, and Platform Admins, see your request and approve or deny it. When it's approved, you are a **member** of the workspace, and it appears in your workspace selector. To be an admin there, ask an owner to change your role.

## Follow your requests

The **My requests** tab lists the requests you sent, with their status: **Pending**, **Approved**, **Denied**, **Withdrawn** or **Expired**. Each shows the workspace's name as it was when you sent the request.

- **Withdraw…** in a pending request's menu cancels it. You can send a new one later, as you can after a denial.
- A request expires if nobody decides it within 7 days.
- If you are added to the workspace another way while a request is pending, the request can no longer be approved.

You can have up to 20 pending requests, and send up to 5 in a minute and 50 in 24 hours. Past that, the dashboard says **Too many requests** and when you can try again.

## Accept an invitation

An owner or admin can invite you by email. You get an invite code, by email if it is set up or from the person who invited you.

1. Sign in with single sign-on.
2. Open **Accept invitation** (`/invitations/accept`), from the email's link or by typing the address.
3. Enter the code.

The code works only for the email address it was made for: your signed-in, verified email must match. It expires after 3 days and works once. See [Invitations](/docs/using/workspace-settings#invitations).

## If you can't find a workspace

- **No discoverable workspaces** means no Team or Project is open to requests. Ask a workspace owner to add or invite you.
- A link to a workspace you aren't in opens **Home** with **Workspace not found or not available**.
- If an admin just added you, choose **Reload my access** in the account menu.
