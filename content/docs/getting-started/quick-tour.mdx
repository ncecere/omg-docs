A walk around the Open Model Gateway dashboard, so the rest of these docs make sense.

## Signing in

The dashboard has one way in: **Sign in with single sign-on**, through your organisation's identity provider. There are no local passwords and no development login.

<Screenshot slot="sign-in" />

Signing in only proves who you are. If you have no platform role yet, the page says **No access yet** and asks you to contact a platform admin. Once you have a role, your first sign-in also creates your personal workspace.

A dashboard session ends after 2 hours without activity, or 12 hours after you signed in, unless your installation set other limits; then you sign in again. See [Your profile](/docs/using/your-profile#how-long-a-session-lasts).

## Two portals

Everyone sees the **Workspace** portal. Platform Admins and Auditors also see a **Portal** switch at the top of the sidebar, with **Workspace** and **Admin**.

### Workspace

The sidebar has two groups:

- **Workspace**: **Home**, your own summary across every workspace you belong to: the models you can use, your usage this month (spent, requests, tokens) and your API keys.
- A group named after the **selected workspace**, with the pages for that workspace: **Overview**, **Models**, **API keys**, **Logs**, **Files**, **Usage & costs** and **Settings**. Pages you can't use there are not shown.

The workspace selector at the top lists your workspaces under **Personal**, **Teams** and **Projects**. You only see workspaces you are a member of; a platform role never adds entries to it.

### Admin

The Admin portal is for the whole installation. Auditors see the same pages read-only, marked **Platform auditor: Configuration and totals, read-only**.

| Group | Pages |
|---|---|
| (top) | **Overview**: a setup checklist and the installation at a glance |
| People | **Users**, **Teams**, **Projects**, **SSO groups** |
| Models | **Connections**, **Models**, **Catalogs** |
| Usage & spend | **Usage & costs**, **Logs**, **Cost centers** |
| Records | **Audit log**, **Legal holds**, **Key safety** |
| Settings | **General**, **Defaults & limits**, **Data & privacy**, **Email**, **Alerts**, **Audit export**, **Sign-in** |

## Everywhere

- **Search**: press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> to jump to any page or workspace you can open. Search never looks inside requests or keys.
- **Notifications**: the bell in the top bar shows how many alerts are unread. See [Alerts and notifications](/docs/using/alerts-and-notifications).
- **Your account menu** (bottom of the sidebar): **Your profile** (your workspaces, where you're signed in, and a download of your data; see [Your profile](/docs/using/your-profile)), **Reload my access** (after someone changes your roles), the theme (**System**, **Light**, **Dark**) and **Sign out**.
- **Links are shareable.** Every page has its own address. If someone sends you a link to something you can't see, the page says so instead of showing it.

## Who sees what

| | Personal workspace | Team or Project member | Team or Project admin | Platform Auditor | Platform Admin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Own keys and requests | Owner only | Yes | Yes | Yes (own) | Yes (own) |
| Others' requests in the workspace | No one | No | Yes | No | No |
| Workspace cost totals | Owner | Own only | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Change workspace limits | Platform only | No | Tighten only | No | Yes |
| Installation settings | | | | Read | Read and change |

A Platform Admin who is not a member of a Team sees its configuration but not its keys or requests. To work inside a Team, an admin needs a membership like anyone else.

## Phones

The dashboard works at phone width: the sidebar becomes a menu, and page headers collapse their secondary actions into a **⋯** button.
